Our Academic Approach

Education that connects knowledge with practical skills.

Iyunga Technical Secondary School provides secondary education that combines academic knowledge with scientific, technical and practical learning.

Our approach aims to help students develop strong academic foundations while also acquiring the skills needed to understand, analyse and solve real-world problems.
