About Our School

Excellence in academic, scientific and technical education.

Iyunga Technical Secondary School

Iyunga Technical Secondary School is a historic government-run boys' technical institution located in Iwambi, Mbeya City Council, Tanzania.

The school combines academic education with technical, scientific and practical learning, preparing students with knowledge and skills for further education, employment and national development.
