Who We Are

A historic technical secondary school in Mbeya, Tanzania.

Iyunga Technical Secondary School

Iyunga Technical Secondary School is a historic government-run boys' technical institution located in Iwambi Ward, Mbeya City Council, Tanzania.

The school provides integrated academic, scientific, technical and practical education. Its educational approach aims to equip students with knowledge, practical skills, discipline and the ability to solve real-world challenges.

Our History

A long-standing tradition of technical and academic education.

Iyunga Technical Secondary School has a long educational history dating back to the early twentieth century. The institution was originally established around 1925 during the German colonial period.

During the subsequent British administration, the institution continued to develop its educational role before gaining formal post-independence registration in 1963.

Since then, Iyunga has continued to contribute to secondary education in Tanzania, with a particular identity in technical, scientific and practical education.

The school's long history has contributed to its recognition as one of Tanzania's legacy technical secondary institutions.
